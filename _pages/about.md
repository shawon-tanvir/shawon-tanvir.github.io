  <h2 class="department__title">Research</h2>

  <div class="article-grid">
    <div class="copy">
      <p class="dropcap">I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at George Mason University, working under Dr. Kevin Lybarge. My research centers on health informatics: building and evaluating conversational AI systems for safety in mental-health care. I also work in natural language processing, explainable AI, and computer vision.</p>

      <p class="fields-line">Fields: <em>Health Informatics</em> · <em>Natural Language Processing</em> · <em>Large Language Models</em> · <em>Explainable AI</em> · <em>Computer Vision &amp; GANs</em> · <em>Semi-Supervised Learning</em> · <em>Image Processing</em></p>

      <blockquote class="pull-quote">I always make an effort to encourage students to carry out in-depth research and explore cutting-edge technologies.</blockquote>

      <p>Currently under review: <em>A Patient Simulation Framework for Risk Assessment of Conversational Healthcare AI: Evaluation of an Antidepressant Decision Aid</em>, submitted to <a href="https://ai.jmir.org/">JMIR AI</a> and posted on <a href="https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.11391">arXiv</a>.</p>
