  <h2 class="home-section__title">About</h2>
  <p>I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at <a href="http://gmu.edu/">George Mason University</a>, working under <a href="https://www.gmu.edu/profiles/klybarge">Dr. Kevin Lybarge</a>. My research centers on health informatics — building and evaluating conversational AI systems for safety in mental-health care — alongside natural language processing, explainable AI, and computer vision.</p>
  <p>I was formerly a Lecturer at <a href="http://aust.edu/">Ahsanullah University of Science and Technology (AUST)</a>, Dhaka, Bangladesh, in the <a href="https://www.aust.edu/cse">Department of CSE</a>, where I also completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering, graduating first in my class. I always make an effort to encourage students to carry out in-depth research and explore cutting-edge technologies.</p>
</section>
